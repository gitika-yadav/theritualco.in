1 kg is a comfortable start for Pilates, barre and toning flows. 2 kg gives you more room to progress if you already train. Many people keep both.

Care & Colours

How do I clean silicone and rubber pieces?
Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap, then let dry fully before storing. Avoid harsh solvents and soaking.
How should I store my pieces?
Somewhere cool and dry, away from direct sun and heat, which can fade colours and age silicone and rubber.
What colours do you offer?
