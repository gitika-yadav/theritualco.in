Our story

Equipment worth keeping in plain sight.

Most workout gear is built to be put away: loud colours, hard plastics, logos everywhere. We wanted the opposite, pieces that feel good in the hand and look good in a living room, so the mat stays rolled out and the weights stay within reach.

The Ritual Co. began with one object, a capsule-shaped silicone dumbbell. It has since grown into a full collection for mat Pilates and everyday movement: mats, ankle weights, Pilates rings and balls, yoga blocks, belts and grip socks, plus curated kits that match piece for piece.

Everything is designed by us in Delhi and made in one of four shades, so it all works together.

The founder

Gitika Yadav

Gitika is a software engineer at Google and a fitness content creator. She has worked with Cult.fit and featured on Radio City India's Beyond Muscles series. She started The Ritual Co. because she couldn't find equipment she was happy to leave out at home.

She brings an engineer's eye to how each piece should feel in the hand, and a creator's eye to how it should look in a room.

"I wanted tools that make you want to show up, not ones that sit in a corner."
