Tula Move vs The Ritual Co: Which Design-Led Fitness Brand Fits You?

Tula Move has become one of the most popular design-led Indian fitness brands — best known for the Balance Block, Balance Ball, Reform Ring and The Grip Yoga Mat. If you've been comparing Tula Move products against what else is out there, you've likely come across The Ritual Co too.

Both brands sit in the same new category: aesthetic, pastel-toned fitness equipment designed to live in your home, not hide in a cupboard. This guide compares Tula Move vs The Ritual Co product by product so you can decide which fits your practice and your space.

Product-by-Product Comparison

Balance Block vs The Ritual Block

Tula Move's Balance Block is a yoga block used for support and alignment in poses. The Ritual Co offers the Ritual Block — a dense EVA foam yoga block in a similar dimension class (23 cm × 15 cm × 10 cm), available in pastel colourways. Perfect for deepening stretches, seated poses and balance work.

Balance Ball vs The Ritual Ball

The Balance Ball from Tula Move works for core, stability and pilates. The Ritual Co's Ritual Ball is a 25 cm mini pilates ball made of non-toxic, anti-burst PVC — the standard studio size for core, inner-thigh and pelvic-floor work.

Reform Ring vs The Ritual Ring

Tula Move's Reform Ring is a pilates ring for resistance training. The Ritual Co's Ritual Ring is a 14-inch pilates magic circle with dual foam-padded handles and studio-standard resistance, matched to the same colour palette.

The Grip Yoga Mat vs The Ritual Mat

The Grip Yoga Mat is Tula Move's flagship mat. The Ritual Co's Ritual Mat uses a natural rubber base for non-slip grip with a sweat-resistant top layer — a 5 mm, 183 cm × 61 cm mat built for yoga, pilates and floor work.

Strength Straps vs The Ritual Belt

Tula Move sells Strength Straps for stretching and mobility. The Ritual Co's Ritual Belt is an organic-cotton yoga strap with an adjustable metal buckle — for deeper stretches, alignment and posture work.

Yoga / Pilates / Therapy Kits vs Ritual Bundles

Tula Move curates Yoga Kit, Pilates Kit and Therapy Kit packs. The Ritual Co offers Ritual Kits — a choice of pilates and yoga bundles that pair the ball, ring, block, cuffs, belt and mat at a bundle price.

Power Pods, Booty Bands and Form Bands

Tula Move also carries Power Pods, Booty Bands and Form Bands. The Ritual Co keeps its collection focused — if you're comparing weighted pods or rings for lower-body work, The Ritual Cuffs (adjustable silicone ankle weights) are the closest fit for adding gentle resistance to pilates and barre.

Which should you choose?

If you want the broadest catalogue and cool-toned colours, Tula Move is a strong option. If you prefer a small, cohesive collection in warm pastels — with skin-safe materials, natural-rubber grip, free shipping above Rs 999 and a wholesale programme — explore The Ritual Co collection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who makes Tula Move?

Tula Move is an Indian D2C brand selling design-led pilates and yoga accessories like the Balance Block, Balance Ball, Reform Ring and The Grip Yoga Mat. The Ritual Co is an Indian D2C brand in the same space, offering the Ritual Block, Ritual Ball, Ritual Ring and Ritual Mat in soft, pastel colourways.

Is Tula Move good quality?

Tula Move is a popular aesthetic fitness brand in India with a loyal following. Buyers looking for alternative design-led home fitness gear also evaluate The Ritual Co, which uses skin-safe, non-toxic materials across a similarly curated range.

What is the difference between Tula Move and The Ritual Co?

Both are premium design-first Indian fitness brands. Tula Move carries a wider catalogue including bands and straps, while The Ritual Co keeps a smaller curated range — dumbbells, mat, belt, block, cuffs, ball, ring and kits — with a consistent pastel aesthetic and a wholesale programme.

Where can I buy pilates equipment like Balance Ball or Reform Ring in India?

Tula Move sells its own Balance Ball and Reform Ring. The Ritual Co offers equivalent equipment — The Ritual Ball (25cm pilates ball) and The Ritual Ring (pilates magic circle) — available online in India with free shipping on orders above Rs 999.

What is a Reform Ring used for?

A Reform Ring — also called a pilates ring or magic circle — is used for resistance training in pilates, targeting the inner and outer thighs, arms, chest and core. The Ritual Ring is a studio-standard 14-inch pilates ring with dual foam handles.

Is there a wholesale option from Indian pilates brands?

Yes. The Ritual Co offers wholesale pricing on orders of 10 or more units of any single product, for gyms, studios, resellers, trainers and corporate programmes. Tula Move also lists a wholesale page on its site.
