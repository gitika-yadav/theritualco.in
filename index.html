Questions

When will my preorder ship?

Preorder items (the Mat, Cuffs, Ball and all kits) ship within 2–3 weeks. You get an email the moment yours is dispatched, and delivery then takes 2–5 working days.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

Yes, across India, for a ₹200 handling fee. You can also pay online.

What is your return policy?

Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for unused items in their original condition.

Can I choose different colours inside a kit?

Each kit ships in one colourway so every piece matches. Choose Black, Cream, Pink or Coffee on the kit page.

Is the silicone safe for skin?

Yes. Our dumbbells use soft-touch, skin-safe silicone over a cast-iron core.

Do you ship across India?

Yes, pan-India. Shipping is free on orders above ₹999.
