Barre Arm Workout: 10 Minutes with Light Weights

By Gitika Yadav, founder of The Ritual Co · 28 November 2026 · 5 min read

The arm section is many people's favourite part of a barre class. The weights are tiny, the moves look easy, and two minutes later your shoulders are on fire. Here is a 10-minute version you can do at home.

What weights to use

Go lighter than you think. Barre arms use long sets and small, continuous movement, so 0.5–1kg hand weights or 2 lb wrist weights are plenty. You can use either:

  • Wrist weights like The Ritual Cuffs (2 lb each, adjustable). Your hands stay free, which suits circles and pulses.
  • Light dumbbells like our 1kg capsule dumbbells, which suit curls, presses and rows.

No weights yet? Do the whole routine with empty hands first. It is harder than it sounds.

Form cues

  • Stand in a small plié or with soft knees, core gently engaged.
  • Shoulders down and back throughout; do not let them creep up to your ears.
  • Move slowly. Control on the way down matters as much as the lift.
  • Keep breathing, especially during pulses and holds.

The 10-minute barre arm workout

8 moves · 1 minute each · then stretch

1. Arm circles1 min

Stand in a small plié, arms out to the sides at shoulder height, palms down. Make small forward circles for 30 seconds, then backward circles for 30 seconds.

2. Overhead pulses1 min

Arms straight up, slightly in front of your head. Pulse them an inch further up, keeping your shoulders away from your ears.

3. Biceps curls with a hold1 min

Elbows tucked at your sides. Curl the weights up slowly, then lower halfway and hold for 2 seconds before finishing the rep.

4. Triceps kickbacks1 min

Hinge forward slightly with a long spine, elbows bent and tucked by your ribs. Straighten your arms behind you, squeeze, and bend back in.

5. Chest presses1 min

Arms in a goalpost shape in front of your face, elbows at shoulder height. Squeeze the forearms together, then open them a few inches.

6. Lateral raises1 min

Arms by your sides. Lift them out to shoulder height with soft elbows, then lower slowly. Finish with 15 seconds of small pulses at the top.

7. Upright rows1 min

Hold the weights in front of your thighs. Draw your elbows up and out to shoulder height, keeping the weights close to your body, then lower.

8. Hug-a-tree1 min

Arms rounded in front of your chest as if hugging a tree. Open the arms wide, then close them back in, keeping the curve in your elbows.

Stretch2 min

Take the weights off. Cross one arm across your chest and hold, then reach one hand down your back for a triceps stretch. Repeat on the other side.

How to fit it into your week

Do it on its own two or three times a week, or add it to the end of our 20-minute barre routine or ankle weight leg workout for a full-body session. For more arm work with dumbbells, see dumbbell exercises for arms.

If you are pregnant, recovering from an injury or have a health condition, check with your doctor or physiotherapist before starting a new workout.

Frequently asked questions

What weight should I use for barre arms?

Light: around 0.5–1kg hand weights or 2 lb wrist weights. Barre arm sets are long and continuous, so heavier weights usually break your form.

Can I do barre arms without weights?

Yes. The same moves with empty hands are still challenging because of the long sets and pulses. Add light weights once you can finish every set with good form.

Are wrist weights or dumbbells better for barre?

Both work. Wrist weights keep your hands free for circles and pulses; light dumbbells suit curls, presses and rows. Many people use wrist weights for barre and dumbbells for other strength work.

How often should I do a barre arm workout?

Two or three times a week, with a rest day in between, is enough for most people.
