Fabric vs Silicone Ankle Weights: Which Should You Buy?

Ankle weights are having a moment — the small, wearable load that turns leg lifts, side-lying work and barre movements into genuinely toning exercises. But once you start looking, you hit one fork in the road immediately: fabric or silicone?

Here's the honest comparison, built around how these weights are actually used for pilates and barre.

Fabric ankle weights

Pros:

  • Soft and familiar — feels like a padded cuff
  • Sometimes cheaper at entry price
  • Available in more colours and prints

Cons:

  • Absorb sweat — they get damp during a pilates session, take time to dry and develop odour
  • Velcro straps wear out and catch on the fabric
  • Lose shape and stiffness after washing
  • Cleanliness is a real maintenance burden when you train daily

Silicone ankle weights

Pros:

  • Waterproof — sweat beads up and wipes off in seconds
  • Skin-safe, non-irritating and easy to sanitise between users
  • Stay supple and hold their shape for years
  • Weight is distributed evenly and stays put while you move
  • Look great left on a shelf — the aesthetic reason the design-led brands chose silicone

Cons:

  • Slightly higher price
  • Can feel unfamiliar if you're used to plush fabric cuffs

The verdict

For sweat-heavy, regular pilates and barre work, silicone wins — waterproof, wipe-clean, skin-safe and durable. Fabric weights make sense only if a plush feel matters more to you than maintenance or hygiene.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are silicone or fabric ankle weights better?

For pilates, barre and low-impact workouts, silicone is better: it's waterproof, easy to wipe clean, skin-safe and doesn't absorb the sweat that makes fabric weights degrade and smell over time.

Do ankle weights actually help with toning?

Yes, when used carefully. 1-2 lb per ankle adds meaningful resistance to leg lifts, side-lying work and barre without heavy load. Keep reps slow and controlled so you use muscle, not momentum.

What weight should I start with?

Start with 1-2 lb per ankle. The Ritual Cuffs are 2 lb per cuff, sold as a pair, with an adjustable strap so you can fine-tune the fit.

Can I wear ankle weights while walking?

Light ankle weights (1-2 lb per ankle) add a small calorie burn to walking, but don't wear heavy weights for long walks — they strain the knees and change your gait. They're best saved for pilates, barre and leg-work exercises.
