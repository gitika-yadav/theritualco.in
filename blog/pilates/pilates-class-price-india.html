How Much Does a Pilates Class Cost in India? City Prices for 2026

Pilates has gone from a niche studio workout to something every Indian metro now has a waitlist for. What nobody publishes in one place is what it actually costs. Studios price in single classes, 8-class packs, 14-class packs and monthly memberships, so comparing them is harder than it should be.

We collected the publicly listed prices for 33 pilates studios across Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad and Kolkata and turned every pack and membership into one comparable number: what you pay per class.

Key findings

  • Mumbai is the most expensive city for a single class: ₹1,000 to ₹2,750, with a median of ₹1,500 across eight studios.
  • Kolkata is the most affordable: single classes from ₹500, and class packs as low as ₹333 per class.
  • Hyderabad studios sell by the month: listed memberships range from ₹6,000 to ₹24,499 a month, with a median of ₹12,000.
  • A class pack saves roughly a fifth to a quarter: where a studio listed both, packs cut the per-class price by 18% to 29%.
  • On a pack, most cities converge on ₹750–₹1,100 per class.

The median listed membership is ₹12,000 a month. At three classes a week, that works out to roughly ₹900–₹1,000 per class, in line with a pack price in the other cities.

How much does a class pack actually save?

Every studio that listed both a single-class price and a pack gave a discount for committing. Some examples from the listings:

  • A Mumbai reformer studio: ₹2,750 for one class, ₹2,250 each on a 12-class pack (18% less).
  • Another Mumbai studio: ₹1,500 for one class, ₹15,000 for 14 classes, which is ₹1,071 each (29% less).
  • A Kolkata studio: ₹699 for one class, ₹6,000 for 12, which is ₹500 each (28% less).

If you're going twice a week or more, a pack pays for itself within the month. If you're still deciding whether pilates is for you, ask the studio whether it offers a trial class first.

Why the prices vary so much

  • Reformer or mat. Reformer classes need a machine per person, so classes are small and prices are higher. Mat classes sit at the bottom of every range.
  • Class size. Studios running three or four people per class charge more than those running ten.
  • Instructor and brand. Studios led by well-known instructors or physiotherapists price at the top of their city's range.
  • Neighbourhood. Within a city, rents shape prices as much as anything else.

How we collected the data

We gathered every pilates studio price we could find publicly listed for each city, from LBB's city guides, and checked them on 28 November 2026. We counted each studio once. Where a studio listed a pack or a membership, we divided the price by the number of classes to get a per-class figure. Medians are shown only where at least seven studios listed that kind of price. Prices change and studios run offers, so always confirm the current price with the studio before you sign up.

Bengaluru, Pune and Chennai are missing because too few studios there publish their prices to give a fair picture. We'll add them as more studios do.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a pilates class cost in India?

In the studio listings we compared, a single group pilates class costs ₹500–₹1,200 in Kolkata, ₹1,000–₹1,500 in Delhi and ₹1,000–₹2,750 in Mumbai, where the median is ₹1,500. Buying a pack of 8–12 classes usually brings the price down to roughly ₹750–₹1,100 per class.

Which Indian city has the cheapest pilates classes?

Of the four cities we compared, Kolkata had the lowest listed prices: single classes from ₹500–₹699 and class packs working out at ₹333–₹875 per class. Mumbai had the highest single-class prices.

Is a pilates class pack worth it?

If you go at least twice a week, yes. In the studios where both prices were listed, a class pack cut the per-class price by about 18% to 29% compared with paying for single classes.

Why is reformer pilates more expensive than mat pilates?

Reformer classes need a machine for every person, so classes are small and the studio's equipment cost is high. Mat classes can take more people on cheaper equipment, which is why mat drop-ins sit at the lower end of the ranges.
