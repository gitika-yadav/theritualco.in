Wholesale Discount

A wholesale discount applies when you purchase 10 or more units of any single product. For example, if you buy 10 pairs of Capsule Dumbbells, every pair in that order is eligible for the wholesale discount on dumbbells.

The same rule applies to every item we sell — be it The Ritual Mat, The Ritual Belt, The Ritual Block, The Ritual Cuffs or The Ritual Ball. As long as an item's quantity in your order is 10 or more, that item is eligible for the wholesale discount.

How It Works

  • Choose at least 10 units of any single product in your order.
  • The wholesale discount is applied to every unit of that product.
  • Items ordered in quantities below 10 units are charged at their regular retail price.
  • For quantities above 10, get in touch with us for the best possible bulk pricing.

Who Can Buy Wholesale

Wholesale pricing is open to anyone purchasing in bulk — retail stores, online resellers, fitness and pilates studios, gyms, corporates, NGOs and individual bulk buyers.
