Choosing a Pilates or Yoga Kit

Not sure which kit is right for you? Start with how you practise, what equipment you already own, and how much resistance or support you want.

What is included in a Pilates kit?

A Pilates equipment kit can combine several pieces used for strength, control, mobility and stability. Depending on the kit, this may include a Pilates mat, Pilates ring, Pilates ball, dumbbells, ankle cuffs and other supporting equipment.

If you are starting Pilates at home, a beginner-friendly kit gives you multiple ways to train without having to purchase every piece separately.

Which Pilates kit is best for beginners?

The Pilates Starter Kit is designed as an entry point for home Pilates. It combines a mat, ring, ball and 1kg dumbbells, giving beginners the basic equipment needed to add resistance and variety to their practice.

What is included in a yoga kit?

A yoga equipment kit commonly includes a yoga mat and props such as a yoga belt and yoga blocks. These props can help with alignment, support, flexibility and accessibility during different poses.

For a complete home setup, the Yoga Essentials Kit combines a mat, belt and block. If you already own a mat, the Yoga Studio Kit provides the essential props without another mat.

Why buy a fitness equipment kit?

Buying a curated kit can make it easier to build a consistent home workout setup. Instead of choosing individual pieces, you can start with equipment designed to complement the same style of practice.

The Ritual Co. kits are designed around intentional movement, combining functional fitness equipment with a considered aesthetic for the home.
