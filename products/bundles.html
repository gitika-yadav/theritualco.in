The Ritual Guide

Choosing your Pilates or yoga kit

Start with how you practise, what you already own, and how much resistance or support you want. Here's how to choose.

  1. What's in a Pilates kit?

    A home Pilates kit gives you everything a mat class uses. A mat grounds you. A Pilates ring and a Pilates ball add resistance for inner thighs, arms and core. Light 1kg dumbbells or ankle weights turn slow, controlled movement into strength work.

    Every Ritual Co. Pilates kit is built from those pieces, so each one gives you a complete workout with no reformer.

  2. Which Pilates kit is best for beginners?

    The Pilates Starter Kit: mat, ring, ball and 1kg dumbbells. It covers the fundamentals (core, control and light resistance) without anything you won't use yet. When you want more, add ankle weights or move up to the Pilates Complete Kit, which adds the cuffs and a block.

  3. Already have a mat?

    Skip paying for another one. The Pilates Sculpt Kit brings the ring, ball, 1kg dumbbells and ankle weights to the mat you own. For yoga, the Yoga Studio Kit is a belt and two blocks, the pair of props most studio classes use.

  4. What's in a yoga kit?

    A mat that grips, a block for support and a belt for reach. The Yoga Essentials Kit pairs our 5mm natural rubber mat with an EVA foam block and a cotton yoga belt, so you can hold poses with good alignment while your flexibility catches up. See all yoga equipment.

  5. Why buy a kit instead of separate pieces?

    Two reasons. A kit costs less, saving you up to ₹2,195 on the Pilates Complete Kit. And every piece arrives in the same colour, so your practice corner looks considered rather than collected.

  6. Pilates or yoga?

    Pilates builds deep core strength and control through small, precise movements. Yoga builds flexibility, balance and breath. Many people do both, and the mat, block and belt work for each. Read Pilates vs yoga for the full difference.

Pilates & yoga kit FAQs

Which is the best Pilates kit for beginners?

The Pilates Starter Kit (₹8,799) is made for beginners starting Pilates at home. It includes The Ritual Mat, Ring, Ball and a pair of 1kg Capsule Dumbbells: everything you need for a full mat Pilates session, with no reformer.

What should a Pilates kit include?

A mat to work on, a Pilates ring and a Pilates ball for resistance and control, and light weights (1–2kg dumbbells or ankle weights) to add strength work. A block is useful for support as your practice deepens. You don't need a reformer to do Pilates at home.

Which yoga kit is best for beginners?

The Yoga Essentials Kit (₹5,799): a natural rubber yoga mat, a cotton yoga belt and an EVA foam yoga block. The block and belt help with alignment and reach while your flexibility builds.

Can I buy a Pilates or yoga kit without a mat?

Yes. The Pilates Sculpt Kit (ring, ball, 1kg dumbbells and ankle weights) and the Yoga Studio Kit (a belt and two blocks) are both made for the mat you already own.

How much do I save by buying a kit?

Every kit costs less than buying its pieces separately: ₹198 on the Yoga Studio Kit, ₹598 on Yoga Essentials, ₹997 on Pilates Sculpt, ₹1,197 on Pilates Starter and ₹2,195 on Pilates Complete.

Do the pieces in a kit match?

Yes. Each kit comes in one colour: Oat Latte, Strawberry Milk, Vanilla Cream or Onyx Black. Strawberry Milk yoga kits come with a Vanilla Cream belt.

Do you ship kits across India?

Yes. Shipping is free on every order across India, and cash on delivery is available. Kits that include a piece on pre-order show the expected ship date on the kit page.

Do I need a reformer to do Pilates at home?

No. Mat Pilates with a ring, ball and light weights gives a full-body workout at home without the cost or space of a reformer. Our Pilates for beginners guide shows how to start.
