Will it slip when my hands get sweaty?
No. The open-cell PU top absorbs sweat rather than letting it pool, so grip holds as you warm up, even in hot, humid sessions.

Why does it cost ₹4,999?
Most mats in the ₹1,000–2,000 range are PVC or TPE foam: light, slippery when wet, and they curl and wear out quickly. The Ritual Mat is a natural rubber base with a PU top: denser, grippier and made to last for years, which is why it's covered by a 1-year warranty.

Is it heavy to carry?
It's about 2.8 kg. That weight is what keeps it flat and in place while you move, and it's still easy to roll up and carry to class.

Does it smell when new?
Natural rubber has a light, natural scent when new. Unroll it and let it air for a day or two and it fades.

Will the lighter colours stain?
Like any PU-top mat, it can pick up marks from oils, lotions and dirty feet. Wipe it after each session and avoid body lotion before practice. Vanilla Cream shows marks most, Onyx Black least.

Is 5 mm enough for my knees?
5 mm balances cushioning with stability, so you feel supported without sinking. For kneeling work, fold the end over for extra padding.

Is it latex-free?
No. The base is natural rubber, which contains natural latex. If you have a latex allergy, please don't use this mat.

Yes, COD is available across India, and shipping is always free.
