Ballet-inspired strength for your living room. A chair for a barre, soft weights, a small ball and grip socks, in four calm colours.

Barre borrows the positions of ballet (pliés, relevés and leg lifts) and pairs them with Pilates-style core work. Most of it is done standing, holding something steady for balance, with small movements repeated until your legs shake. It is low impact, needs no dance experience and fits into a few square feet of your home.

You do not need a studio or a ballet barre. The back of a sturdy chair is enough. What makes the difference is a handful of small props: grip socks so you do not slide, a 25cm ball to squeeze, and light ankle and wrist weights to turn every lift into a burn. New to it? What you need for barre at home

  • Something to hold: a sturdy chair, counter or windowsill at about hip height.
  • Grip socks: to stay steady in pliés and relevés on smooth floors.
  • A 25cm ball: squeezed between the thighs or knees to switch on the inner thighs.
  • Light ankle and wrist weights: around 2 lb (0.9 kg) each for leg lifts and arm work.
  • A mat: for the core and glute section at the end.
