Kosha Yoga vs The Ritual Co: Which Aesthetic Fitness Brand Fits You?

Kosha Yoga Co is one of the most recognised premium yoga brands in India — founded by yoga teachers in Mumbai, loved for its natural-rubber mats, cork blocks and sustainability story. If you've compared Kosha products, you've probably seen The Ritual Co in the same searches for aesthetic home fitness equipment.

Both brands design the kind of equipment you'd keep out in the open. But they serve different priorities: Kosha leans premium-yoga-and-sustainability, while The Ritual Co is an aesthetic pilates-and-strength brand at accessible prices. Here's the honest product-by-product comparison.

Product-by-Product Comparison

PUre Yoga Mats vs The Ritual Mat

Kosha's flagship is the PUre natural-rubber mat, with a sweat-absorbent microfibre top, in 4.5mm and 6.5mm thicknesses, priced from about ₹6,499–6,999. The Ritual Co's Ritual Mat is a 5mm natural-rubber grip mat at ₹4,999 — 183cm × 61cm, with a sweat-resistant top layer, built for yoga, pilates and floor work. Kosha wins on print personalisation and extra thickness; the Ritual Mat gives you studio-standard natural-rubber grip at a lower price, and fits pilates as closely as it fits yoga.

Cork Yoga Blocks vs The Ritual Block

Kosha's blocks are 100% natural cork — the "Balance Block" semi-circle brick and standard cork blocks, from roughly ₹999–1,299. Cork is grippy and sustainable but heavy, sweat-absorbing and hard under the hands. The Ritual Co's Ritual Block is a dense EVA foam block at ₹899 in the standard 23cm × 15cm × 10cm size — light, wipe-clean and kind on the wrists, and just as stable for seated poses and balance work.

Kosha Cuffs vs The Ritual Cuffs

Both brands sell silicone ankle and wrist weights — a real overlap. Kosha Cuffs are fixed bangle-style weights at ₹3,999. The Ritual Co's Ritual Cuffs are ₹3,499, 2 lb per cuff with an adjustable, waterproof silicone strap — a closer fit for pilates leg lifts, barre and side-lying work where you want the weight to stay put.

Yoga Belt vs The Ritual Belt

Kosha offers a yoga belt for stretching work. The Ritual Co's Ritual Belt is an organic-cotton yoga strap with an adjustable metal buckle, priced at ₹499 — a budget-friendly alternative for deeper stretches and alignment work.

Pilates overlap: where they differ most

Kosha is fundamentally a yoga, meditation and sustainable-apparel brand. For pilates specifically, its range only reaches props and weights. The Ritual Co, in contrast, is built around pilates — a 14-inch pilates ring, a 25cm anti-burst pilates ball, a pilates mat, block, cuffs, belt and light dumbbells in one cohesive pastel palette.

Which should you choose?

Kosha Yoga Co is the right pick if you want a premium, publication-backed yoga brand, love printed tactile mats, and value the sustainability story enough to pay for it. The Ritual Co is the better fit if you train pilates and yoga at home and want studio-standard equipment — natural-rubber grip, correct pilates sizing — at accessible prices (₹499–₹4,999), with free shipping above ₹999 and a wholesale programme for studios.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who makes Kosha Yoga Co?

Kosha Yoga Co is an Indian brand founded in 2017 by yoga teachers in Mumbai. It designs premium, sustainability-led yoga and meditation equipment — natural rubber and printed mats, cork blocks, meditation cushions and yoga wear.

Is Kosha Yoga Co good quality?

Yes. Kosha's PUre mats are made from natural rubber with sweat-absorbent tops, are teacher-recommended and have been featured in publications like Vogue India and Grazia. They're premium products — and their prices reflect that, with mats starting around ₹6,499.

What is the difference between Kosha Yoga Co and The Ritual Co?

Kosha is primarily a premium yoga, meditation and sustainable-apparel brand with an emphasis on natural materials, while The Ritual Co is an aesthetic pilates and strength brand — ring, ball, mat, block, cuffs and light dumbbells in pastel colourways — with accessible pricing starting at ₹499, free shipping above ₹999 and a wholesale programme for 10+ units.

How are Kosha Cuffs different from The Ritual Cuffs?

Both are silicone ankle and wrist weights, but they're built differently. Kosha Cuffs are bangle-style fixed weights priced at ₹3,999. The Ritual Cuffs are 2 lb per cuff with an adjustable fit, marked at ₹3,499 — suited to pilates, barre and leg-lift work.

Which is better for pilates: Kosha Yoga Co or The Ritual Co?

For pilates, The Ritual Co is the more direct match — it builds pilates-specific equipment like the 25cm Ritual Ball and 14-inch Ritual Ring alongside a mat, block, cuffs and belt. Kosha is chiefly a yoga and meditation brand, so its pilates overlap is limited to ankle weights and props.

How much do Kosha Yoga mats cost compared to The Ritual Mat?

Kosha's natural rubber PUre mats are priced from approximately ₹6,499 to ₹6,999. The Ritual Mat is ₹4,999 — a 5mm natural rubber grip mat with a sweat-resistant top layer, 183cm x 61cm, also built for pilates.
